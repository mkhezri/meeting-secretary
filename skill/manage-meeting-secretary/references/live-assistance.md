# Private live meeting assistance

## Prepare for questions
Before the meeting build a compact index of the private pack: topic/question, source ID and actual locator, version, retrieved time, effective/as-of date, permissions, unresolved conflicts and relevant specialist. Include approved decision boundaries and known calculations with inputs/units. Never invent access, cached content, prices or a live feed. Preserve the participant agenda as a separate, less-sensitive artifact.

An interactive agent can answer when invoked. Continuous listening, automatic recording, capture of participants and a background service are separate capabilities requiring operational evidence and applicable consent. Do not imply this package enables them.

## Answer in the meeting
1. Identify the question and intended audience. If visibility is unknown, do not expose restricted pack details on a potentially shared screen. Offer a non-sensitive answer or ask to move the response to an authorized private view. Permission to read is not permission to show participants.
2. Use the indexed pack first, then retrieve only the needed authorized evidence. For changed or time-sensitive facts recheck the original accessible source. State both the data's as-of date and retrieval time where they differ. A recent retrieval does not make an old price or organization chart current.
3. Route a bounded technical question through the available specialist in specialist-routing.md. Preserve formula, inputs, currencies, units, dates, assumptions and source references. Mixed contract terms, wet/dry tonnes and calendar systems must be reconciled before calculation. If a required input is absent, provide a conditional calculation only when explicitly labeled and useful; no invented “actual” result.
4. Give the shortest useful answer: conclusion, source/version or pinpoint, uncertainty/contradiction, next clarification if needed. If retrieval takes time, first state the verified part and say what is being checked. Do not improvise to meet a response-time target.
5. An unsupported answer is “not verified with available evidence.” Conflicting evidence is presented as conflicting, including the authority/effective date of each source. Confidence scores do not substitute for source evidence.
6. A suggested answer, scenario or negotiation recommendation remains a proposal. Only separately evidenced meeting agreement or owner approval can change a decision/action register. Do not send, schedule, promise, purchase, assign or modify records merely because a live answer suggests doing so.

## Q&A and disputes
When persistence is authorized, record question_id, meeting_id/revision, question time, question text, asker attribution if known, intended audience, answer/classification, source IDs/versions/pinpoints, data as-of/retrieval time, specialist/calculation inputs, unresolved/disputed points and approval/retention scope. Redact unnecessary personal information. Without authorized storage, provide a transient draft for owner review; do not claim it was saved.

Never silently change the source transcript or original answer. A correction links the prior answer, new evidence and reason; revise affected minutes for approval. A Q&A log is evidence of what was answered, not proof that the answer was accepted or became a meeting decision.

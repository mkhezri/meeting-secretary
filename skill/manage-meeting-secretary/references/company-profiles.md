# Source-backed company, role and project profiles

Resolve company_id and project_id first; never borrow another company's template, staff, figures or approval hierarchy because it looks similar. Profile fields:
- organization chart: source locator/version, issue/effective date, approving authority if evidenced, departments/reporting lines and unresolved vacancies;
- role: verified person, current role, responsibilities, decision/approval limits and evidence; a job title alone grants no authority;
- project: contract/version, scope, stakeholders, milestones, constraints, prior decisions and source links;
- template: document type, source file/version, owner/approval evidence, effective date, allowed use, font/size, RTL/LTR, margins, headers/logos, numbering, tables/signatures and fixed wording, each observed rather than guessed;
- freshness: retrieved_at, data_as_of, last verified, conflicts and status (verified, candidate, missing, superseded).

Read only the relevant profile and sources. Owner corrections supersede an outdated note within their stated scope, but preserve contrary signed source evidence for clarification rather than silently rewriting it. Missing profile fields remain unknown; prepare focused questions instead of an invented chart.

## Format acceptance
A historical issued document can be a candidate example, not automatically the current approved template. Verify company, business domain, meeting type and the complete participant-organization set; ask/retain a blocker if official currency or approval is not established. An approved source template is reused without replacing its corporate identity or fixed text. Extract actual formatting, render the new output and compare side by side for fonts, sizes, margins, structure, signatures and RTL. State any unavailable font/rendering substitutions and obtain approval before declaring exact conformance.

Record source ID/version, template status, last verification, derived-output ID and visual review result. No actual corporate template is supplied merely by the existence of the generic content outline.

## Scoped visual-template registry
Follow [template-registry.md](template-registry.md). Never use company identity alone as a template key. Treat internal commerce and foreign commerce as different domains. Keep owner visual approval, exact minutes-revision content approval and recipient/data-sharing permission as separate records. No context is preapproved by this distribution.

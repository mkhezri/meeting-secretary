# Portable specialist routing

Use relevant available specialists or skills, not hard-coded personal integrations. Load only what the current question needs. Check actual availability; if absent, analyze permitted sources within competence or request qualified review. Do not claim certification or imply a specialist ran when it did not.

| Topic | Capability to select | Expected contribution |
|---|---|---|
| Earlier decisions and commitments | Authorized knowledge-source search | Provenance, coverage, freshness and contradictions |
| Operations and project delivery | Relevant operations/project specialist | Dependencies, milestones and missing evidence |
| Amounts, quantities and contracts | Appropriate calculation specialist | Versioned inputs, reproducible formulas, units and assumptions |
| Trade or multicurrency matters | Relevant commercial specialist | Contract-specific currencies, price bases, dates and risks |
| Legal or tax interpretation | Qualified review where appropriate | Attributable review of actual governing documents |
| Calendars, documents and messages | Available artifact/communication adapters | Verified destinations, formatting and operation results |

Apply during preparation, live Q&A, minutes review and follow-up. Split mixed questions into bounded subquestions; reconcile currencies, calendars, units and source versions. Never carry a sample contract's figures or counterparties into another project. A newer informal note does not automatically override a signed source.

Supply the question, minimum permitted evidence, assumptions, units/calendar, expected output and ambiguities. Record sources and reconcile conflicting contributions. Specialist conclusions are recommendations, not authority to change contracts, assign work or execute obligations. Communicating with an external agent requires explicit permission for that recipient and data scope; internal routing does not authorize external disclosure.

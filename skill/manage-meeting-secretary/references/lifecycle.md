# Full meeting lifecycle

## Intake and readiness
Create or reconcile one stable meeting ID. Check for an existing event/register before creating another. Record original calendar/date expression and timezone; don't assume a Persian date means Gregorian. Resolve relative dates from the actual meeting context, not processing time. Record intended audience, company/project, sensitivity and objective. A tentative meeting is distinct from a confirmed slot.

## Before the meeting
Search relevant authorized sources with their modification dates and coverage. Identify previous unresolved commitments, pending responses, source conflicts, and decisions requested. Missing or stale evidence must appear in the preparation pack rather than being silently filled with assumptions.

Prepare two separately approved deliverables as separate files/exports. The combined content worksheet is for authoring only and must never be distributed. Inspect participant exports, comments and metadata for private content before sending:
- Private manager pack: context, source links, risks, negotiation boundaries, unresolved questions, decision alternatives and facilitation notes. Never attach this to participant messages by default.
- Participant agenda: approved title, time/place/link, attendees, objectives, timeboxed topics, required pre-reads, expected decisions and preparation requests. Include only information authorized for those recipients.

Use templates/meeting-pack-en.md or templates/meeting-pack-fa.md for content, not as a substitute for a company's official layout. First match company, business domain, meeting type and participant organizations in the scoped registry; for a new or mismatched context obtain owner approval of its first visual variant. Obtain the current approved template and inspect text styles, fonts, size, margins, headers, logos, numbering and approval fields. Preserve fixed legal or company text. Render and visually verify formatted deliverables using the relevant artifact skill. No template available: state that exact-format conformity is blocked, deliver a usable content draft, and continue independent work.

After agenda approval, resolve participant identities and the selected channel; send only approved data. Record attempted/service-accepted/delivered/acknowledged evidence separately. Prepare a manager facilitation plan matching the approved agenda with topic timeboxes, questions, decision gates and action capture points.

## During and after
Recording requires the applicable consent and permitted processing route. If there is no recorder, support the session from notes rather than claiming capture. Preserve actual timestamps and uncertain speaker labels. Keep source versions and correction annotations immutable; a specialist's interpretation is a recommendation rather than replacement evidence.

Produce decisions and action register with source pointers. Separate promises actually made from recommended follow-up. Ask about ambiguous material commitments. Identify required financial, technical or legal review and keep its conclusions attributable to that reviewer/source.

Present minutes, proposed dates and acceptance criteria for approval. Apply approvals per object/revision. Then execute the authorized routing/scheduling plan using available adapters. Lack of a platform API is a blocker to automatic project routing, not permission to simulate success.

## Change and cancellation
A changed time, attendance, venue, agenda or decision creates an affected revision. Keep the old record and reason. Check previously sent messages, calendar IDs and scheduled reminders before applying an authorized update. Calendar acceptance does not approve changed business decisions. Cancellation stops only linked authorized reminders/tasks whose purpose ended; unrelated commitments remain open. Send change/cancellation notices only to approved affected recipients through permitted channels. Verify provider state; hold uncertain outcomes for reconciliation.
